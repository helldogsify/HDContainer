- **Box width and gap with sliders, applied at once.** In **Settings → Boxes → Arrange** the width and the gap have a slider and a field; the boxes on the desktop change while you move them, before Save (Cancel puts everything back). The place you picked (along the top, along the bottom or in a column) is remembered and highlighted, so the gap and width keep it; moving a box by hand ends that.
- **Boxes along the bottom.** A new **Along the bottom: left / center / right**. A box in the lower half of the screen opens upwards, with its title as a footer; it grows up smoothly while the title stays put.
