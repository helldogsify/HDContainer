- **Desktop boxes.** Panels on the desktop that hold files, folders and shortcuts — under your windows, there after Win+D. Drag files in and out (to the desktop, other boxes, any app), double-click to open, right-click for the full Explorer menu. Boxes roll up into their title and open on hover, show a counter of new files, have their own color and transparency, and can sort new desktop files by type. Double-click the empty desktop to hide them. Make one from the tray menu (**New box**); **Settings → Boxes** holds the sorting rules and imports your boxes from iTop Easy Desktop.
