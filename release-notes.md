- **Smoother boxes.** Rolling a box open or closed now runs at about 60 frames per second instead of 4–5 frames, the first frames of the animation no longer drop out, and the box changes size together with its picture. The highlight under the mouse follows within a frame. Boxes open 50 ms after the cursor arrives and roll up a bit sooner after it leaves.
- **The bottom of a box follows the text.** When the last row has a two-line name, the box ends below the text with the same margin as above the icons; rows leave room for two-line names.
