## Key scripts: instant on/off notice

- The notice for a script's on/off hotkey now reacts at once: every press replaces the notice on screen instead of waiting in a queue behind the previous one, so you always see the current state.
- New short animation: a switch inside the indicator, green with the knob on the right for "on", grey with the knob on the left for "off" (0.12 s), next to the script name. It disappears after 1.4 s.
- During a dictation only the latest on/off notice waits for it to end; older ones are dropped.
