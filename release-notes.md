## Screen recording

- New: record a region of the screen or the whole screen into an MP4 video. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Y** (or tray → «Record video»), drag a region or click for the whole monitor, then choose on the panel under the region: computer sound, microphone, mouse pointer, click rings — and press «Record».
- While recording, a red pill above the clock shows the time (click = stop) and a thin frame marks the region. Both are hidden from screen capture: they are on your screen, never in the video.
- Stop with the same hotkey, from the tray menu or by clicking the pill. The video goes to the Desktop (or the folder set in Settings → Screen recording).
- H.264 + AAC, hardware encoder when the graphics card has one; 15 / 30 / 60 frames per second, normal or high quality.
- If the app is closed or crashes during a recording, the video is recovered at the next start.
