- **Screenshots.** Press **Ctrl+Shift+A** (any key or combination can be set in Settings → Screenshots) and the screen freezes. Drag to select a region, or just click to take the whole screen; Esc cancels. The capture opens in a small editor: pen, marker, frame, arrow and crop, six colors, three line widths, Ctrl+Z to undo. **Copy to clipboard** (or Enter) closes the editor with the result in the clipboard; **Save as…** writes a PNG or JPEG file. The editor can be skipped so the capture goes straight to the clipboard.
- **Screenshot history.** Every capture is kept on your PC for 30 days (7 to 90 in Settings), like the dictation history. Tray → **Screenshot history** shows them as thumbnails: copy one again, open it in the editor or delete it.
